Frequently asked questions

Is TaskPe only for WhatsApp work?

No. WhatsApp tasks remain supported, alongside other microtask and consumer research categories that may be available on the platform.

How do earners get started?

Create an account, open the task list, choose an available task, and follow its instructions. Requirements and rewards are task-specific.

How do businesses request work?

Share your campaign brief with the TaskPe team and we can discuss structured testing, feedback, research, and data tasks.
